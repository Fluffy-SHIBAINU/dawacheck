# DawaCheck pitch (11 slides)

Slide titles as they appear in the deck (https://claude.ai/artifact/VFmE5yFdgihQEJL9oXrimJ).

1. **DawaCheck.** World Bank Small AI for Development · Health. Subtitle: "Check a Medicine Against Nigeria's NAFDAC Register With No Internet. Photo In, Verdict Out, in Hausa, English or Pidgin." Screenshot of the app's home screen in Hausa.
2. **Fake Medicine Kills, and Rural Nigerians Buy From Shops With No Pharmacist.** 1 in 10 medical products in LMICs is substandard or falsified (WHO). Up to 169,000 child pneumonia deaths a year are linked to bad antibiotics. 75% of rural Nigerians first go to a medicine shop with no pharmacist. 2.2 billion people are offline.
3. **Built for People Who Buy Medicine Where Doctors Are Scarce.** Amina (caregiver, Kano, Hausa), Musa (medicine vendor, Zaria), Ngozi (community health worker, Enugu), and the NAFDAC pharmacovigilance desk.
4. **Photo In, Verdict Out, All on the Phone.** Photograph → read (OCR on the phone; sideways photos are turned) → check against 8,922 registered products and 84 NAFDAC alerts stored offline → green / amber / red, read aloud in Hausa, English or Pidgin → report saved offline and synced when a signal returns. Find by name and the alert list work offline too.
5. **Three Verdicts, From Real Demo Cartons.** Screenshots: green (Artheget EZ), amber (copied number, wrong strength), red (NAFDAC alert, in Pidgin).
6. **Small AI in Numbers.** 19.0 MB for the whole offline app (4.8 MB register of 8,922 products, 10.9 MB on-device OCR, 2.2 MB recorded voice, 0.5 MB app code). About 1.5 seconds per photo. No GPU, no cloud, no per-check cost.
7. **Works Offline, Gets Smarter Each Time It Connects.** Community flags (3 reports from 2 phones), OCR corrections, coverage gaps for NAFDAC, fresher packs. Opt-in, no personal data, designed for NDPA 2023.
8. **Real Data, Real APIs, Tested.** NAFDAC Greenbook (8,922 products). NAFDAC public alerts (84 since Jan 2025), with brands and 307 batch numbers extracted by Claude. ElevenLabs voices in Hausa, English and Pidgin. Supabase sync, live. 223 unit tests and 22 end-to-end tests.
9. **Meet the Team.** Shawn Yoon, founder (photo from the team video), plus Claude (Claude Code and a crew of AI agents). Shawn worked on DCPS, the Disability Case Processing System: a seven-year, $700M U.S. national project. Quote from the team video: "When my family first arrived, we stood in a pharmacy aisle unable to read a single medicine label. Frustrating, but it wasn't deadly for us. For millions of people, it is deadly." Claude Code and the agents built and tested the app; Claude extracted the NAFDAC alert data.
10. **Path to Scale.** Pilot in Kano with patent medicine vendors and community health workers. NAFDAC partnership: a live register and alert feed, and an SMS shortcode for reports. Native Android app. More countries with the same pack format (Kenya PPB, Ghana FDA).
11. **Future Roadmap.** Today: the live web app. Next: a three-month pilot in Kano with 50 medicine vendors and community health workers, with introductions to NAFDAC and two state ministries of health. Then: native Android, a live NAFDAC register feed, and Yoruba and Igbo reviewed by native speakers. Future: a standalone DawaCheck device with DawaCheck preinstalled, no phone needed, for families, pharmacies and clinics (concept render, AI-generated). Try it now: dawacheck-smoky.vercel.app.
